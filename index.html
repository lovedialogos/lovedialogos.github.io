Clinical Psychotherapist and owner of Love Psychotherapy, LLC

Love C. Dialogos is a licensed marriage and family therapist serving Madison, Wisconsin, and clients by telehealth across the 13 states where Love is licensed.

Most of Love's work is with adults who were misdiagnosed for years and are identifying as autistic or ADHD later in life. Love also works with couples and with survivors of narcissistic and antagonistic abuse.

Credentials: AAMFT Approved Supervisor and Clinical Fellow. Texas Board-Approved Supervisor. Certified Autism Spectrum Disorder Specialist (ASDCS). ADHD-CCSP. Certified Narcissistic Abuse Treatment Clinician (NATC).
